Chilled Shrimp Cocktail

~ A classic seafood appetizer featuring tender chilled shrimp served with a bold homemade cocktail sauce. ~Serves 4

Prep Time: 10min

Chill Time: 20min

Ingredients:

For The Shrimp:

  • 1 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined with tails on
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
  • Water, for boiling
  • Ice, for ice bath

For the Cocktail Sauce:

  • 1/2 cup ketchup
  • 2 tablespoons prepared horseradish
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon hot sauce
  • Fresh cracked black pepper, to taste

Instructions:

  1. Fill a large pot with water and add lemon slices, garlic, salt, and peppercorns. Bring to a boil.
  2. Add shrimp and cook for 2–3 minutes, or until pink and opaque.
  3. Immediately transfer shrimp to an ice bath to stop the cooking process. Chill for 5 minutes, then drain and refrigerate.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together ketchup, horseradish, Worcestershire sauce, lemon juice, hot sauce, and black pepper.
  5. Arrange chilled shrimp on a serving platter with lemon wedges and cocktail sauce.
  6. Serve cold!

CHEF'S TIPS:For the best texture, avoid overcooking the shrimp. They should be firm, tender, and slightly crisp when chilled.
