Classic Cannoli

~ A traditional Sicilian pastry featuring crisp golden shells filled with sweet ricotta cream and finished with chocolate chips and powdered sugar. ~Serves 12

Prep Time: 35min

Chill Time; 1hr

Cook Time: 20min

Ingredients

For the Cannoli Shells:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 3 tablespoons cold butter, cubed
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup Marsala wine
  • Vegetable oil, for frying

For the Filling:

  • 2 cups whole milk ricotta cheese, drained
  • 3/4 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/3 cup mini chocolate chips

For the Garnish:

  • Powdered Sugar
  • Chopped Pistachios
  • Additional Chocolate Chips

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, cinnamon, and salt.
  2. Cut in cold butter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  3. Add egg and Marsala wine and mix until a dough forms.
  4. Knead dough for 3–4 minutes until smooth. Wrap and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  5. Roll dough very thin and cut into 4-inch circles.
  6. Wrap dough circles around cannoli tubes and seal edges lightly with water.
  7. Heat oil to 350°F and fry shells for 2–3 minutes until golden brown and crisp.
  8. Remove carefully and cool completely before removing metal tubes.
  9. For the filling, combine ricotta, powdered sugar, vanilla, cinnamon, and chocolate chips until smooth.
  10. Refrigerate filling for at least 30 minutes.
  11. Pipe filling into cooled cannoli shells just before serving.
  12. Garnish with powdered sugar, pistachios, or extra chocolate chips.

CHEF'S TIP:Filling the cannoli just before serving keeps the shells crisp and prevents them from becoming soggy.
