Italian Baccalà(Salted Cod) in Tomato Sauce

~A traditional Italian seafood dish featuring tender salt cod slowly simmered in a rich garlic tomato sauce with olives, capers, and fresh herbs. A classic centerpiece for the Feast of the Seven Fishes. ~Serves 4

Soak Time: 24-48hrs

Prep Time: 20min

Cook Time: 40min

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds salted cod (baccalà)
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • 1/4 cup Kalamata olives, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons capers
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh cracked black pepper, to taste

Instructions:

  1. Rinse salted cod under cold water and place in a large bowl. Cover with cold water and refrigerate for 24–48 hours, changing the water every 8 hours to remove excess salt.
  2. Drain cod and pat dry with paper towels. Cut into serving-sized pieces.
  3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat.
  4. Add onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in garlic and crushed red pepper flakes and sauté for 1 minute.
  6. Pour in white wine and simmer for 2 minutes.
  7. Add crushed tomatoes, olives, capers, and Italian seasoning. Stir well.
  8. Nestle cod pieces into the sauce and simmer gently for 25–30 minutes until tender and flaky.
  9. Finish with fresh parsley and black pepper before serving.
  10. Serve hot with crusty Italian bread or roasted potatoes.

CHEF'S TIP:Taste the cod after soaking before adding extra salt to the dish. Properly soaked baccalà should remain flavorful but not overly salty.
