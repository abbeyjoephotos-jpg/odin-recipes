Christmas Struffoli Tree

~ A traditional Neapolitan holiday dessert featuring crisp bite-sized dough balls coated in warm honey and decorated with colorful sprinkles. A beloved Christmas treat often served alongside other desserts during Italian holiday celebrations. ~ Servings: 10

Prep Time: 30min

Cook Time: 20min

Ingredients:

For the Dough:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • Pinch of salt

For Frying:

  • Vegetable oil, for frying

For the Glaze:

  • 1 cup honey
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon fresh orange juice

Garnish:

  • 2 tablespoons rainbow nonpareil sprinkles
  • 1/4 cup candied orange peel, chopped (optional)
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting (optional)

Instructions:

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine flour and salt.
  2. Add eggs, sugar, melted butter, vanilla extract, and lemon zest. Mix until a dough forms.
  3. Transfer dough to a lightly floured surface and knead for 3–4 minutes until smooth.
  4. Cover and let rest for 20 minutes.
  5. Roll dough into ropes approximately 1/2 inch thick and cut into small pieces about the size of a marble.
  6. Heat vegetable oil in a deep pot to 350°F.
  7. Fry dough pieces in batches for 2–3 minutes until golden brown.
  8. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels.
  9. In a large saucepan over low heat, combine honey, sugar, and orange juice. Stir until smooth and warmed through.
  10. Add fried dough balls to the honey mixture and toss gently until evenly coated.
  11. Transfer to a serving platter and shape into a mound or wreath.
  12. Decorate with sprinkles and candied orange peel.
  13. Allow to cool slightly before serving.

Chef's Tip: For authentic flavor, use high-quality honey and avoid overheating it. Warm honey coats the struffoli beautifully while maintaining its delicate sweetness and shine.
