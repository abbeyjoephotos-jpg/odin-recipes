Whole Roasted Branzino with Lemon & Herbs

~ A classic Mediterranean seafood dish featuring tender roasted branzino infused with garlic, lemon, olive oil, and fresh herbs. ~Serves 4

Prep Time: 20min

Cook Time: 30min

Ingredients:

  • 4 whole branzino, cleaned and scaled
  • 5 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 6 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 2 lemons, thinly sliced
  • 6 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 4 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • 1/3 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh cracked black pepper, to taste

Directions:

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Pat branzino dry with paper towels and season the inside and outside generously with salt and black pepper.
  3. Stuff each fish cavity with lemon slices, garlic, thyme, and rosemary.
  4. Arrange fish on a lightly oiled baking sheet or large roasting pan.
  5. Drizzle olive oil evenly over the fish.
  6. Roast for 25–30 minutes, or until the flesh is opaque and flakes easily with a fork.
  7. Remove from oven and garnish with fresh parsley and additional lemon slices.
  8. Serve immediately with roasted vegetables, potatoes, or a light salad.

CHEF'S TIP:Scoring the skin with a few shallow diagonal cuts before roasting helps the fish cook more evenly and allows the herbs and seasoning to penetrate deeper into the flesh.
