Bruschetta Appetizer

~A fresh and simple Italian appetizer with toasted bread, ripe tomatoes, garlic, basil, and olive oil.~Serves 6

Prep Time: 15min

Cook Time: 10min

Ingredients:

  • 1 French baguette, sliced into 1/2-inch pieces
  • 4 ripe Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 clove garlic, peeled whole (for rubbing bread)
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic glaze
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh cracked black pepper, to taste

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Arrange baguette slices on a baking sheet and lightly brush each slice with olive oil.
  3. Toast bread for 6–8 minutes, or until lightly golden and crisp around the edges.
  4. In a mixing bowl, combine diced tomatoes, minced garlic, chopped basil, olive oil, balsamic glaze, salt, and black pepper.
  5. Remove toasted bread from oven and immediately rub each slice lightly with the whole garlic clove.
  6. Spoon tomato mixture onto each slice just before serving.
  7. Garnish with additional basil or balsamic glaze if desired.

CHEF'S TIP:For the best flavor, let the tomato mixture rest for 10 minutes before serving so the ingredients fully combine.
