Lamb Cutlets w/ Tabbouleh and Tahini Sauce

Succulent Mediterranean-style lamb cutlets marinated with garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, and aromatic herbs, then grilled until perfectly tender and lightly charred for rich, savory flavor. Served alongside a vibrant rice tabbouleh made with fluffy rice, fresh parsley, diced tomatoes, cucumbers, mint, and a bright citrus dressing, this dish delivers a refreshing balance of hearty and fresh ingredients. Finished with creamy tahini sauce and warm Mediterranean spices, every bite combines smoky, herbaceous, and zesty flavors for an elegant and satisfying meal inspired by coastal Greek and Middle Eastern cuisine.

Ingredients:

  • Finely grated zest and juice of lemon.
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes(optional).
  • 2 garlic cloves crushed.
  • Half cup(60ml) extra virgin olive oil.
  • 8 French-trimmed lamb cutlets.
  • 500g Cooked rice, cooled(we used microwave brown and wild rice).
  • 1 1/2 cups(180g) frozen baby peas, thawed.
  • 3 spring onions, thinly sliced.
  • 150g cherry tomatoes,quartered.
  • 1/2 bunch flat-leaf parsley, leaves picked, plus extra leaves to serve.
  • 1/2 bunch mint chopped, leaves picked, plus extra leaves to serve

Green Tahini Sauce:

  • 3/4 cup(200g) Greel-style yoghurt.
  • 1/4 cup(70g) Hulled tahini
  • 1/2 bunch flat-leaf parsley, leaves picked
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon
  • 1/2 garlic clove, crushed

Cooking Directions:

  1. For green tahini sauce, place all ingredients into blender, blitz until smooth, and season. Pour into container and chill.
  2. In a bowl, combine lemon juice, chilli flakes, garlic and oil. In annother bowl, place lamb and pour over half of lemon mixture. Turn to coat. Marinate for 15 minutes
  3. Preheat a bbq and grill over high heat. Meanwhile, place rice, peas, spring onion, and tomatoes in bowl with remaining lemon and chilli mixture. Toss to combine.
  4. On the bbq grill, cook lamb for 2 minutes on each side or until cooked to your liking. Rest on plate loosely covered with foil. Place rice tabbouleh on plate with 2 lamb cutlets.
