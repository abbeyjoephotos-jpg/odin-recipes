Southern Style Beef Stew Served Over a Bed of Rice

~ Rustic beef stew prepared with slow-simmered beef, potatoes, and carrots in a seasoned southern-style gravy, served atop a bed of white rice. A filling and satisfying dish perfect for cozy family dinners. ~Serves 6

Ingredients:

  • 1-1 1/2lb beef stew meat cut into two inch pieces or smaller
  • 3tbsp olive oil
  • 2tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup red wine
  • 1 onion peeled and chopped
  • 3 carrots - peeled and sliced
  • 3 celery stalks - sliced
  • 1lb potatoes - rinsed
  • 4 cups all-natural beef stock
  • 1 sprig of fresh rosemary
  • 1tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Cracked black pepper to taste
  • 1/2 cup fresh chopped parsley, and extra for garnish

Ingredients for Rice:

  • 4 cups of water
  • 2 cups of long-grain white rice, or bismati rice
  • 1tsp salt
  • 2tbsp butter

Steps of Preperation:

  1. In a large saucepot or Dutch oven, heat olive oil, and butter
  2. Open package of beef chunks and cut each piece of meat into halves. They usually come in large pieces and you can cut each piece into half, this will cut the cooking time down.
  3. In a small dish, place the flour.
  4. Dip each piece of meat into the flour and coat all sides. No need to dust off excess flour
  5. Drop each piece of flour-coated meat into the pot with the melted butter and oil. Raise the heat to medium heat and cook the meat to brown all sides, about 5-7 minutes
  6. Pour in the red wine and simmer on l ow heat for 2 to 4 minutes, allowing the wine to reduce a little. With a wooden spoon scrape the bottom of the pot to loosen up the flour.
  7. Then add the chopped onion, celery, and carrots, seasoning, fresh rosemary, fresh chopped parsley, and beef stock. Raise heat to medium-high
  8. Bring to low boil, then lower the heat to lowest, and simmer for about 35 to 45 minutes, uncovered. Stirring occasionally.
  9. Taste the gravy and season with salt and pepper to taste. If you desire, you may add 1 or 2 teaspoons of cornstarch or flour to thicken the gravy. Pull out 1 piece of meat to test.
  10. Meantime, in a medium size pot, make the rice, following the directions on the package.
  11. Add potatoes for last 15 minutes.

Rice Preperation:

  1. In a medium-size pot, bring 4 cups of water to boil. Add rice, salt, and butter. Cover, simmer on the lowest heat for 20 minutes, or until cooked to tender.
  2. When rice is ready, spoon some rice into serving dishes or pasta bowls, then create a well in the center of the rice and fill with stew.
  3. Serve immediately
