Stuffed Calamari in Tomato Sauce

~ A traditional Italian seafood dish featuring tender squid stuffed with seasoned breadcrumbs, herbs, garlic, and Parmesan cheese, slowly simmered in a rich tomato sauce. A classic addition to the Feast of the Seven Fishes. ~Serves 4

Prep Time: 25min

Cook Time: 45min

Ingredients:

For the Calamari:

  • 1 1/2 pounds whole squid (calamari), cleaned with tentacles reserved
  • 1 cup Italian-style breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 egg
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh cracked black pepper, to taste

For the Sauce:

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • Fresh basil, for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Finely chop the reserved squid tentacles.
  2. In a skillet over medium heat, sauté chopped tentacles in olive oil for 2–3 minutes. Remove from heat and allow to cool slightly.
  3. In a mixing bowl, combine breadcrumbs, Parmesan cheese, garlic, parsley, egg, cooked tentacles, salt, and black pepper.
  4. Carefully stuff each squid tube about three-quarters full and secure the openings with toothpicks.
  5. Heat olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat.
  6. Add sliced garlic and crushed red pepper flakes and sauté until fragrant.
  7. Pour in white wine and simmer for 2 minutes.
  8. Stir in crushed tomatoes and bring to a gentle simmer.
  9. Add stuffed calamari to the sauce, cover partially, and simmer gently for 30–35 minutes until tender.
  10. Garnish with fresh basil and serve with crusty Italian bread or pasta if desired.

CHEF'S TIP:Do not overstuff the calamari, as the filling expands slightly while cooking and may cause the squid to split.
