Italian Braised Eel in Tomato Sauce

~ A traditional southern Italian seafood dish featuring tender eel slowly simmered in a rich garlic tomato sauce with herbs and olive oil. Commonly served during the Feast of the Seven Fishes. ~Serves 4

Prep Time: 20min

Cook time: 40min

Ingredients:

  • 2 pounds eel, cleaned and cut into 3-inch pieces
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh cracked black pepper, to taste
  • Fresh basil, for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Pat eel pieces dry with paper towels and season lightly with salt and black pepper.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat.
  3. Add eel pieces and lightly brown on all sides for 4–5 minutes. Remove and set aside.
  4. In the same pan, add onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in garlic and crushed red pepper flakes and cook for 1 minute.
  6. Pour in white wine and simmer for 2 minutes to reduce slightly.
  7. Add crushed tomatoes and Italian seasoning. Stir well.
  8. Return eel to the sauce, cover, and simmer gently for 25–30 minutes until tender.
  9. Finish with fresh parsley and basil before serving.
  10. Serve with toasted Italian bread or over polenta if desired.

CHEF'S TIP:Eel becomes tender and flavorful when cooked slowly. Avoid boiling rapidly, as gentle simmering helps maintain its delicate texture.
