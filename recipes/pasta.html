Spaghetti and Meatballs

A comforting Italian classic featuring tender, hand-rolled meatballs simmered in a rich tomato sauce and served over perfectly cooked spaghetti. Finished with fresh herbs and parmesan, this hearty dish brings authentic homestyle flavor to every bite.

Ingredients:

  • 1lb Lean Ground Beef
  • 1 Egg
  • 1cp Italian Breadcrumbs
  • 2tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil
  • 3-4 Cloves of Finely Cut Crushed Garlic
  • 2tbsp Oregano
  • 3tbsp Dried Basil
  • 2tbsp Thyme
  • 14.5oz Can of Stewed Whole Tomatoes
  • 3oz Tomatoe Paste(optional)
  • Salt and Pepper to Taste
  • 1 Package of Noodles of Your Choice
  • 1 Hungarian Pepper

Steps of Preperation for Meatballs:

  1. Combine egg, ground beef, italian breadcrumbs, 2 cloves of finely cut crushed garlic, 1tbsp oregano, 1tbsp dried basil, 1tbsp thyme, and a pinch of salt and pepper
  2. Roll and lightly press into meatballs
  3. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
  4. place on baking sheet with 1 inch space between
  5. Bake until lightly browned on outside

Steps of Preperation for Sauce:

  1. In saucepan pour 2 tbsp olive oil, bring to heat and add remainder of garlic. Cook to light brown.
  2. Now combine stewed whole tomatoes, remainder of basil, oregano, thyme, and garlic.
  3. Bring to simmer, then reduce heat and continue on low.
  4. Continue to simmer for 1 hour then add meatballs
  5. continue to simmer on low for 2 hours
  6. Add salt and pepper to flavor
  7. Add hungarian pepper and cook for 1 more hour
  8. Now if sauce is to thin add tomatoe paste(optional)
