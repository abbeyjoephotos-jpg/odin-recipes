Classic Tiramisu

~ A rich and elegant Italian dessert layered with espresso-soaked ladyfingers, mascarpone cream, and finished with cocoa powder. ~Serves 8

Prep Time: 30min

Chill Time: 6hrs

Ingredients:

  • 6 egg yolks
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup mascarpone cheese
  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 2 cups brewed espresso, cooled
  • 2 tablespoons coffee liqueur (optional)
  • 1 package ladyfinger cookies
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder, for dusting
  • Dark chocolate shavings, for garnish

Instructions:

  1. In a mixing bowl, whisk egg yolks and sugar together until pale and smooth.
  2. Add mascarpone cheese and mix until fully combined.
  3. In a separate bowl, whip heavy cream until stiff peaks form.
  4. Fold whipped cream gently into the mascarpone mixture.
  5. Combine cooled espresso and coffee liqueur in a shallow dish.
  6. Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the espresso mixture and arrange a layer in the bottom of a serving dish.
  7. Spread half of the mascarpone mixture over the ladyfingers.
  8. Repeat with another layer of dipped ladyfingers and remaining mascarpone mixture.
  9. Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, preferably overnight.
  10. Dust generously with cocoa powder and garnish with chocolate shavings before serving.

CHEF'S TIP:Dip the ladyfingers quickly in the espresso mixture to prevent them from becoming overly soft and soggy.
