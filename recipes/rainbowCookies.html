Tri Layer Rainbow Cookies

~ Italian Rainbow Cookies are a classic Italian-American bakery favorite made with three colorful almond-flavored cake layers, sweet fruit jam, and a rich chocolate coating. Despite their name, they're more like delicate layered cake bars than traditional cookies. Their vibrant red, white, and green layers make them a beautiful addition to holiday dessert trays, special celebrations, or any occasion that calls for an eye-catching sweet treat. ~Makes: 36-48 cookies

Prep Time: 45min

Cook Time: 10-12miin

Chill Time: 4hrs (Or Overnight)

Ingredients:

    For the Cookies:

  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 4 large eggs, separated
  • 2 teaspoons almond extract
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • Red food coloring
  • Green food coloring

For the Filling:

  • ½ cup seedless raspberry jam
  • ½ cup apricot jam

Chocolate Topping:

  • 8 ounces semi-sweet chocolate, melted
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line three 9×13-inch baking pans (or bake in batches using one pan) with parchment paper.
  2. In a large bowl, beat butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Add egg yolks one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Stir in almond extract.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk together flour and salt. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture.
  4. In another bowl, beat egg whites until stiff peaks form. Gently fold the egg whites into the batter.
  5. Divide the batter evenly into three bowls:
    6. Divide the batter evenly into three bowls:
    • Leave one bowl plain.
    • Tint one bowl red.
    • Tint one bowl green.
  6. Spread each batter into a prepared pan and bake for 10–12 minutes, or until the surface springs back when lightly touched.
  7. Allow the layers to cool completely.
  8. Place the green layer on a flat surface and spread with raspberry jam.
  9. Top with the plain layer and spread with apricot jam. Now place red layer
  10. Cover with plastic wrap and place a baking sheet or cutting board on top. Add a few heavy cans to gently compress the layers. Then refrigerate for 4 hrs.
  11. Remove the chilled cookie stack from the refrigerator. Spread half of the melted chocolate over the top. Let it set. Carefully flip the cookie stack and spread the remaining chocolate over the other side. Allow the chocolate to fully harden.
  12. Using a sharp knife, trim the edges and cut into small rectangles or squares.

Chef's Tip: Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week. For the best flavor and texture, allow the cookies to sit at room temperature for about 15 minutes before serving.
