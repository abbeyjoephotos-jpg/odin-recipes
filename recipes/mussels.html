Steamed Mussels Sauted w/ Garlic & Oil

~ Fresh mussels steamed in white wine and finished in a fragrant garlic and olive oil sauce with parsley and crushed red pepper. ~ Serves 4

Prep Time: 15min

Cook Time: 15min

Ingredients:

  • 2 pounds fresh mussels, cleaned and debearded
  • 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh cracked black pepper, to taste
  • Lemon wedges, for serving
  • Crusty Italian bread, for serving

Directions:

  1. Rinse mussels thoroughly under cold water and remove any beards. Discard any mussels that remain open after tapping gently.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet or pot over medium heat.
  3. Add garlic and crushed red pepper flakes and sauté for 1–2 minutes until fragrant, being careful not to brown the garlic.
  4. Pour in white wine and bring to a simmer.
  5. Add mussels to the pan and cover with a lid.
  6. Steam mussels for 5–7 minutes, shaking the pan occasionally, until the shells open.
  7. Remove lid and discard any unopened mussels.
  8. Stir in fresh parsley and season lightly with black pepper.
  9. Serve immediately with lemon wedges and crusty Italian bread for dipping.

CHEF'S TIP:Avoid overcooking mussels, as they can quickly become rubbery. Once the shells open, they are ready to serve.
