Crab Cakes Appetizer

~Golden pan-seared crab cakes blended with Italian herbs, Parmesan cheese, garlic, and fresh lemon for a rich coastal-inspired appetizer or light entrée.~Serves 4

Prep Time: 20min

Cook Time: 12min

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound lump crab meat
  • 1/2 cup Italian-style breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh cracked black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

Aoli Ingredients:

  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely minced

Instructions:

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine crab meat, breadcrumbs, Parmesan cheese, egg, mayonnaise, garlic, parsley, Italian seasoning, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, salt, and black pepper.
  2. Gently fold the mixture together until just combined, being careful not to break apart the crab meat too much.
  3. Form the mixture into 8 evenly sized crab cakes and place them on a plate.
  4. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  5. Cook crab cakes for 4–5 minutes per side, or until golden brown and heated through.
  6. For the lemon aioli, whisk together mayonnaise, lemon juice, and garlic in a small bowl.
  7. Serve warm with lemon wedges and lemon aioli if desired.

CHEF'S TIP:Chilling the crab cakes for 20 minutes before cooking helps them hold together and creates a crispier exterior when seared.
