Chocolate Chip Cookie Cups with Ice Cream

~ Golden-brown chocolate chip cookie cups baked with crisp edges and soft, chewy centers, filled with creamy scoops of your favorite ice cream for the perfect warm-and-cold dessert combination. Each bite blends rich buttery cookie dough, melty chocolate chips, and smooth ice cream into a decadent treat that feels both nostalgic and indulgent. Finished with optional drizzles of chocolate or caramel sauce and topped with whipped cream or sprinkles, these cookie cups are a fun and crowd-pleasing dessert perfect for parties, celebrations, or cozy nights at home. ~Serves 6

Ingredients:

For The Cookie cups:

  • 2 ¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

For The Filling:

  • 1 pint ice cream of your choice (vanilla, chocolate, cookies & cream, or mint chocolate chip work great)
  • Chocolate syrup or caramel sauce (optional)
  • Whipped cream (optional)
  • Extra chocolate chips or sprinkles for topping (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease a muffin tin or use a nonstick muffin pan.
  2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt.
  3. In a large bowl, beat the softened butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until light and creamy.
  4. Add the eggs and vanilla extract, mixing until fully combined.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients and mix until a cookie dough forms. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Scoop about 2 tablespoons of dough into each muffin cup. Press the dough up along the sides slightly to create a cup shape.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the edges are golden brown.
  8. Remove from the oven and immediately use the back of a spoon or a small glass to press down the center of each cookie cup to form a deeper cavity.
  9. Allow the cookie cups to cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
  10. Once cooled, add a scoop of your favorite ice cream into each cookie cup.
  11. Top with chocolate syrup, whipped cream, caramel sauce, or extra chocolate chips if desired.
  12. Serve immediately and enjoy!

CHEF'S TIP:Slightly underbake the cookie cups for a soft, gooey center that contrasts perfectly with your choice of cold ice cream.
