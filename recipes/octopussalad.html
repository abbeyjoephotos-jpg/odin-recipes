Italian Octopus Salad

A light and refreshing Mediterranean seafood dish featuring tender octopus tossed with olive oil, lemon, garlic, parsley, and crisp vegetables.Serves 6

Prep Time: 20min

Cook Time: 45min

Chill Time: 30min

Ingredients:

  • 2 pounds octopus, cleaned
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
  • 1 celery stalk, finely sliced
  • 1/4 cup red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh cracked black pepper, to taste

Instructions:

  1. Fill a large pot with water and add lemon halves, bay leaves, and peppercorns. Bring to a gentle boil.
  2. Carefully add octopus and reduce heat to a simmer. Cook for 40–45 minutes, or until tender when pierced with a knife.
  3. Remove octopus from the pot and allow it to cool slightly.
  4. Cut octopus into bite-sized pieces and place in a large mixing bowl.
  5. Add celery, red onion, parsley, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, salt, and black pepper.
  6. Toss gently until evenly coated.
  7. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving.
  8. Serve chilled or slightly cool with lemon wedges and crusty Italian bread.

CHEF'S TIP:For extra tenderness, allow the octopus to cool in its cooking liquid for 10–15 minutes before slicing.
